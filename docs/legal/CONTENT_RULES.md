# StateUp Community Rules

Be useful, first-hand, and kind.

- Share places you genuinely recommend and use a real, relevant photo you have permission to post.
- Keep comments and chat focused on travel help.
- Never expose private addresses, live travel plans, contact details, or another person’s precise location.
- No harassment, hate, threats, sexual exploitation, graphic violence, scams, impersonation, spam, paid promotion without disclosure, or illegal content.
- Respect land access, closures, wildlife, local communities, and cultural sites. Do not encourage trespassing or unsafe behavior.
- Report content instead of escalating. Blocking hides future content from that user.

Reports enter an admin review queue. Reviewers may dismiss a report, hide content, warn or suspend a user, preserve evidence, or escalate imminent safety threats. Before launch, define reviewer roles, audit logs, response targets, an appeals process, and a child-safety escalation procedure.
