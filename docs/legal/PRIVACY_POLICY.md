StateUp collects the account information a user provides (email, username, display name and authentication-provider identifier), recommendations and photos they post, comments and chat messages, votes, favorites, itinerary items, visited states, friend requests, reports, blocks, push tokens, and security/session records needed to operate the service.

Location access is optional and requested only to show the user on the map, find nearby recommendations, or place a pin. StateUp does not intentionally collect background location. A user chooses the coordinates shared with a public recommendation.

We use information to provide and synchronize the service, authenticate accounts, recover and secure accounts, deliver requested notifications, prevent abuse, moderate user-generated content, respond to support, and satisfy legal obligations. Public profile names, recommendations, selected coordinates, photos, comments, vote counts, and chat messages may be visible to other users. Email, password hashes, session/reset-token hashes, private friend requests, and saved itinerary items are not public.

We may use providers for hosting, databases, object storage/CDN, email, notifications, maps, Apple/Google authentication, diagnostics, and content moderation. Map library assets and map tiles are loaded from their configured providers. When a map is displayed, the tile provider receives requests for the visible map area and standard connection data such as IP address. StateUp does not send the device's precise location to the tile provider as a location value; centering the map near you can still reveal the approximate area being viewed.
