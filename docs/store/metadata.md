## Long description

Every state has a story. StateUp helps you remember where you’ve been and find the places worth pulling over for next.

Check off all 50 states on an interactive map. Open any state to browse photo recommendations from people who know it, from overlooks and old diners to museums, beaches, and wonderfully odd road-trip stops. Save favorites separately from a practical itinerary, open directions in the device’s map app, and connect with friends through private requests.

With StateUp you can:

- Track the U.S. states you have visited
- Discover photo-backed local recommendations
- Add blue pins with precise map locations
- Upvote and comment without an endless feed
- Favorite recommendations and save itinerary stops
- Find and add friends privately
- Use optional location to find nearby ideas
- Report unsafe content and block users
- Permanently delete your account and associated content

Take the long way. The good stops are usually just off the route.
